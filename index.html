Authorship Meter

An at-a-glance scale for how much of a piece of work came from a person and how much from an AI model — built from a structured, LLM-assisted assessment across the stages of the process, not a single guessed number.

It's a voluntary disclosure, not a regulatory filing — see what this is not. This page carries its own Authorship Meter declaration, placed per §8 — intro line here, the meter itself at the foot of the content.

Two more profiles

Provenance and a stale declaration

Method, declarer, and assessed date are always visible — never only in the expanded breakdown. When the work has changed since assessment, the meter says so instead of showing a confidently stale number.

Compact badge

For footers and article bylines. The breakdown expands on click. This one is also this page's own declaration — the anchor the intro line above points to.

The five stages

StageQuestion it answers
ConceptionWhose idea, thesis, or angle is this?
StructureWho decided the architecture, outline, or composition?
ProductionWho made the artifact itself — prose, code, pixels, audio?
CurationWho selected, cut, revised, and shaped the result?
VerificationWho fact-checked, tested, and signed off?

The five levels

LevelNameHuman shareAI share
1 Human only100%0%
2 Human-led, AI-assisted75%25%
3 Co-created50%50%
4 AI-led, human-directed25%75%
5 AI-generated0%100%

Someone has to be accountable for checking the work. There's no "fully AI" option here — a person always signs off, so Verification never reaches level 5.

Using it

<script src="authorship-meter.js" defer></script>

<authorship-meter src="authorship.json"></authorship-meter>

Or inline the declaration in a <script type="application/json"> child. Restyle with --am-human, --am-ai, --am-bg, --am-line, --am-radius.
